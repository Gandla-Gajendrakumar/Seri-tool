What the device did

    Mains

    The 12 V adapter feeds a buck module that makes 5 V. That 5 V charges the cells and, through diode D4, runs the whole system rail VSYS.

    Power cut

    When 12 V disappears, switches Q3/Q12 connect the cells to VSYS with no break, so nothing restarts. The modem always runs straight from the cells (its 2 A bursts need them).

    Brain and signals

    The ESP32 reads the SHT31 probe every 30 s on the I²C bus, keeps time with the DS3231, logs to microSD, talks to the modem through a level shifter and drives the printer, LEDs and buzzer.
